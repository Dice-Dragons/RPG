1.0.0 Beta 1 (2026-11-08)
=========================
* Added standalone RPG domain platform for Genres (xf_cv6_genre) and Publishers (xf_cv6_publisher) with AdminCP management, sorting, and publisher JSON/ZIP export & import.
* Added automatic style property management (cv6Genre group and cv6Genre_<class> properties) for genre pills, badges, and filters.
* Added AdminCP RPG Suite Ecosystem & Add-on Overview (admin.php?rpg/manager) with direct module enable toggles and resource links.
* Added public RPG Suite Dashboard (index.php?rpg/) with left-sidebar Genre filter across all genre-aware widgets.
* Added modular bridge connecting [DD] Character Database, [DD] Dice Roller, [DD] BattleMap Generator, and [DD] Handout Generator (Setting primary wiresets, Campaign Forum wireset provisioning, rollable character fields, character dice sets, campaign content linking, and In-Thread RPG Companion bar).
* Added CLI command php cmd.php dd-rpg:sync-schema and automatic add-on lifecycle listeners for non-destructive schema synchronization.
